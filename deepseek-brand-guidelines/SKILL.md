## Brand Guidelines

### Brand Essence

DeepSeek's visual identity draws inspiration from the concept of "deep exploration"—the relentless pursuit of discovery beneath the surface. The brand centers on a vibrant electric blue that projects innovation, technological optimism, and the boundless potential of artificial intelligence. The iconic blue whale symbol serves as a visual anchor, representing both the "deep" in DeepSeek and the spirit of exploring uncharted territories in AI. Unlike traditional AI brands that pursue abstract symbolism, DeepSeek embraces an accessible, approachable aesthetic that invites users to explore AI's possibilities together.

### Colors

**Primary Color:**

- DeepSeek Blue: `#3C5DFF` (Dodger Blue) - The signature electric blue that anchors the brand, representing technological innovation, forward-thinking vision, and the brand's commitment to democratizing AI.

**Neutral Colors:**

- Deep Black: `#121212` (Cod Gray) - Primary text and dark backgrounds
- Pure White: `#FFFFFF` - Light backgrounds and text on dark

**Contextual Variation:**
- Alternate Blue: `#0D28F3` - For subtle variation when DeepSeek Blue requires additional depth
- Polar White: `#EFF7FB` - For soft, airy backgrounds that complement the blue

### Typography

**Recommended Fonts:**

- **Sans-Serif (Primary)**: Inter, SF Pro, or system-ui - Clean, geometric sans-serif that conveys precision and modernity
- **Sans-Serif (Alternative)**: Helvetica Neue, Arial - For cross-platform fallback
- **Special Character Styling**: DeepSeek's wordmark features distinctive cut strokes where letter strokes meet, creating unique character that remains crisp across small and large displays

**Application Guidelines:**

- **Heading** (24pt+): Bold sans-serif (Inter/SF Pro), letter-spacing: -0.02em to enhance readability at larger sizes
- **Subheadings** (14-23pt): Medium sans-serif, letter-spacing: normal
- **Body text** (10-13pt): Regular sans-serif, line-height: 1.5 for optimal readability
- **Fine print** (8-9pt): Regular sans-serif, line-height: 1.4

### Logo Usage

**Core Symbol:**
The DeepSeek logo features a stylized blue whale, rendered with elegant, flowing curves that convey a sense of dynamic motion—"almost appearing to be jumping across the screen." The rounded, approachable form balances technical sophistication with warmth and accessibility.

**Logo Colors:**
- Standard: DeepSeek Blue (`#3C5DFF`) on White or Light backgrounds
- Light variant: White on DeepSeek Blue
- Dark variant: White on Deep Black (`#121212`)

**Minimum Clear Space:**
Maintain clear space around the logo equal to the height of the "D" in the wordmark. Never place other graphic elements within this zone.

## Features

### Smart Color Application

- Primary brand color is DeepSeek Blue (`#3C5DFF`)
- Use Deep Black (`#121212`) for high-contrast text
- Use Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) for backgrounds or text on dark surfaces
- Apply alternate blue (`#0D28F3`) sparingly for accent depth

### Depth and Motion Cues

DeepSeek's brand suggests the feeling of plunging into deep water:
- Use subtle gradients from light to dark blue to evoke the sense of depth
- Curved, flowing lines suggest whale-like motion and exploration
- Apply whale tail motifs as graphic elements to reinforce brand identity

### Shape and Accent Elements

- Primary shapes should feel organic and flowing, inspired by marine forms
- Subtle whale tail shapes serve as brand accents
- Hexagonal patterns can be used to suggest AI/tech elements, but should complement organic forms rather than overwhelm them
- For non-text elements, DeepSeek Blue should be the dominant accent color

## Technical Details

### Color Formatting

- Hex values: `#3C5DFF` (DeepSeek Blue), `#121212` (Deep Black), `#FFFFFF` (Pure White)
- RGB values: `rgb(60, 93, 255)` for DeepSeek Blue; `rgb(18, 18, 18)` for Deep Black; `rgb(255, 255, 255)` for Pure White
- HSL values: `hsl(230, 100%, 62%)` for DeepSeek Blue
- CMYK values: `76, 64, 0, 0` for DeepSeek Blue; `0, 0, 0, 93` for Deep Black

### Typography Implementation

- Prefer system fonts for consistent rendering across platforms
- Apply `font-family: 'Inter', -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'Segoe UI', Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`
- Headings should use bold weight (700) with negative tracking (-0.02em)
- Body text should use regular weight (400) with standard tracking
- Never stretch or distort the wordmark; maintain proportional scaling
- When using DeepSeek wordmark text, ensure distinctive character cuts are preserved where applicable

### Visual Consistency

DeepSeek's visual identity balances scientific legitimacy with approachability. The design language should feel clean, modern, and confident—projecting the technical sophistication of a cutting-edge AI company while inviting users to explore AI capabilities at depth.
